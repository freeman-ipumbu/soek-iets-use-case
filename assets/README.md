# Public showcase assets

The `social/` directory contains selected SoekIets campaign artwork included to illustrate the public use case:

- `launch-square.png` — 1080 × 1080 launch concept.
- `sellers-portrait.png` — 1080 × 1350 seller-community concept.
- `safe-handover-landscape.png` — 1200 × 628 trust and handover concept.

These assets are demonstration material, not stock photography or an open asset pack. They remain subject to the repository’s [rights notice](../NOTICE.md). The people and scenes are illustrative; they are not testimonials, verified sellers or evidence of completed trades.
